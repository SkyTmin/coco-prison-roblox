Use case: stylized-concept. Original character design sheet for our block-built prison RPG boss, King of the Piglins. Create a polished 2x2 turnaround on warm light grey, no text or labels. Top left strict side facing left; top right strict front; bottom left rear three-quarter; bottom right front three-quarter. Same character and consistent proportions across views. Full body visible with generous margins. Neutral arms-down stance, feet planted at the same baseline in the two orthographic panels.

A massive, greedy boar king about 10 game units tall: heavy round stepped belly, thick neck, broad low snout, small shrewd amber eyes, two ivory tusks, wide floppy ears, a modest battered gold crown with one dark red stone. Short thick legs and split black hooves, muscular forearms, clearly articulated elbows, wrists and fingers. Weight concentrated in the belly and shoulders. Warm dusty pink/brown hide (#AE7A62), shaded burgundy at the snout (#85584C), dark brown leather (#392A23), worn matte ochre gold (#B58A39) with restrained pale edge chips (#D2B069), ivory tusks (#D6C49F), charcoal hoof/steel (#302D29), faded wine cloth (#713B3B).

Asymmetric but practical armor: one overlapping gold shoulder guard, other shoulder in a leather strap, thick segmented wrist guards, waist belt with a square buckle and a coin pouch, short layered loincloth front and back. Back must have a complete rounded silhouette and visible straps, never a flat unfinished slab. In his right hand a heavy single-bladed gold execution axe, short dark haft and a thick chipped wedge-shaped cutting head; clear grip and a usable elbow swing. Left hand open for grabbing a food pouch / summoning. Small curled tail and a dangling belt token for secondary motion.

Everything made of carefully stepped rectangular cuboids, subtle Minecraft-like pixel material grain, deliberately rounded block silhouettes instead of six giant boxes. Studio-quality orthographic lighting, soft ground contact shadow, no bloom, no emissive skin or armor, no glowing outlines, no effects, no environment, no copied commercial boss. This is our own readable silhouette for a mobile Roblox game. The design should suggest real weight, personality and a rig with independently moving belly, chest, jaw, ears, arms, knees, ankles and cloth.
